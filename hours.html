Which case is it?

  1. Sunday — Commercial and Truck entrance only: 16055 Shady Grove Road, Gaithersburg, MD 20877. Hours 9 a.m. – 5 p.m. Sunday is residents only. No commercial vehicles, no businesses, no commercial waste, no commercial or rental vehicles, no loads over 500 lbs. Scales are closed; if the trash load is not clearly under 500 pounds, the county says it will ask you to return Monday. Residential entrance (16101 Frederick Road) is closed on Sundays (and on holidays / other times the Scale House is closed to commercial traffic).
  2. Monday–Saturday, residential (recyclables and/or trash that appears under 500 lb) — Residential entrance: 16101 Frederick Road, Derwood, MD 20855. Hours 7 a.m. – 5 p.m. Proof of Montgomery County residency required. No out-of-county waste.
  3. Monday–Saturday, 500 lb or more of trash, commercial/truck, or a trailer larger than 5 ft × 9 ft (max 24-inch sideboards) — Commercial and Truck entrance: 16055 Shady Grove Road. Hours 7 a.m. – 5 p.m. Loads over 500 lb must be weighed at the scales and pay the required fee. Commercial/business loads must be generated in Montgomery County; contractors must verify job location / county of origin or they will not be permitted to dump.
  4. Tipping floor (main floor), any day you are sent there — Required PPE: high-visibility vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes. No PPE → exit and return with it.
  5. Poolesville instead — Poolesville Beauty Spot, Saturday 8 a.m. – 4 p.m., 19200 Jerusalem Road, Poolesville, MD 20837. Montgomery County residents only; bulk trash under 500 lb / up to 499 lb; no charge. Proof of residency required. Not a full transfer station.

HHW and ECOWISE sit on the Shady Grove campus. They are not a third street address. Resident HHW is on the Shady Grove campus, open daily for residents; confirm the HHW page the morning you go.

ECOWISE (business HHW): Monday–Friday 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. at the HHW compound; no appointment; pay by credit card or business checks to Clean Harbors Environmental Services; no cash. If Transfer Station is closed due to weather, holidays, or emergencies, HHW is also closed. Chemicals not listed / price per pound: call 240-777-6587. Contractors believed to be contractors must register for Ecowise and pay for paint disposal each Wednesday 1–5 p.m.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency for residents: MD State Driver’s license or MD State ID with picture and Montgomery County address; or current utility bill with physical MoCo address and your name; or mortgage statement or lease/rental in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates: the same three documents to show material is from a MoCo address. Bringing material for a resident who is not present: utility bill; mortgage/lease; work order or contract at a MoCo address; letter from the resident (name, address, contact, phone, physical work address); driver’s license with MoCo address and a phone number where the person may be contacted. Rules.
  • All loads must be covered (county code Chapter 48, Section 48-19). Uncovered load: verbal warning, written Notice of Violation, or a citation. Applies to residents, businesses self-hauling, haulers and collectors.
  • Recyclables and trash must be separated; mixed loads not accepted.
  • Vehicles: car, truck, van, or similar. Walking or riding bicycles, motorcycles, or electric scooters are prohibited.
  • Trailers: allowed at residential entrance up to 5 ft × 9 ft, max 24-inch sideboards; larger → Commercial and Truck entrance. No trailers at PUF.
  • Shingles / roofing of any kind: not accepted. Car parts: not accepted. Paper shredding: not offered.
  • Commercial mattresses / box springs (effective Wednesday, 1 July 2026): commercial loads (stake bodies, open tops, etc.) not accepted unless accompanied by a residential manifest verifying same-day collection within Montgomery County; loads without a manifest rejected.
  • Recyclable drop-off accepted from residents and non-residential/commercial (among others): scrap metal / appliances, usable building materials, clothing/shoes, commingled, electronics, bulbs/tubes, HHW from residents, HHW from businesses (fee-based), mixed paper/cardboard, bulky rigid plastics, yard trim up to 6" diameter (fee-based). Accepted only from residents: rechargeable / lead-acid / other non-alkaline batteries, motor oil, antifreeze, propane tanks and compressed gas cylinders, tires.
  • The fees page: “We may ask you to leave a deposit. You will be issued a receipt with your deposit. Once you exit the Scale House, you will either be refunded the full amount of your deposit or charged an additional amount based on the weight of the disposed items.”
  • Payment: debit cards, credit cards including American Express, and corporate accounts. The county does not accept cash and checks.
