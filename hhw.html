Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Home-generated chemicals from a Montgomery County residence (pesticides, solvents, oil-based paint, pool chemicals, batteries, etc.)?

    HHW compound · Recycling Area

    Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station. Enter from Route 355 / Frederick Road; stay right for “Recycling”; blue chain-link compound with a large roof at the top of the hill. Residents, home-generated only. Proof of residency. No extra resident HHW fee. Open during Transfer Station hours of operation. Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only.

  2. 2

    Over 15 gallons of liquid or 75 pounds of solid HHW?

    Call 311 before you go

    How Do I: call Recycling and Resource Management at 311 (or 240-777-0311) to arrange a drop-off. Whether 15 gal / 75 lb is per visit or per month: not stated. Do not invent a monthly cap here.

  3. 3

    Business / contractor / Small Quantity Generator (looks commercial)?

    ECOWISE · fee · Mon–Fri 8–12

    Not free resident HHW. ECOWISE for Montgomery County businesses generating under 220 lb hazardous waste per calendar month and under 2.2 lb acute hazardous waste per month. Same HHW compound. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–12 p.m. No appointment. Register first. Pay per pound (credit card or business check to Clean Harbors; no cash). Credit-card surcharge not to exceed 3%. Pricing questions: 240-777-6587. Paint contractors (or people believed to be contractors): also paid paint disposal Wednesday 1–5 p.m. (HHW facility page) — that is paint-contractor language, not a second full ECOWISE window.

  4. 4

    Over 220 lb hazardous waste / month, or acute hazardous waste over 2.2 lb / month?

    Not ECOWISE · call 311

    ECOWISE is not open to generators above those SQG limits. Where those loads go: not stated on the ECOWISE page. Call 311 (or 240-777-0311). Do not invent a destination.

  5. 5

    TV, computer, or related electronics?

    Electronics pad · not HHW

    HHW refuse list: televisions; computers and related electronics. Same campus, different pad (Recycling Area electronics). See the electronics draft on this site — not this compound.

  6. 6

    Propane, oxygen, CO₂, acetylene, or other compressed gas cylinder?

    Upper lot · not HHW

    HHW refuse list: compressed gas cylinders. Hand tanks to an attendant on the upper lot near electronics (tires/propane page). Prefer return-to-vendor when you can.

  7. 7

    Medical sharps, syringes, or medications?

    Not HHW · see official county page

    HHW How Do I: medical sharps and syringes are not accepted; commercial, industrial, or medical wastes are refused. This unofficial lookup does not give medical or medication disposal advice. Use the official county How Do I pages for sharps and for medicine collection sites — linked under Official sources.

  8. 8

    Ammunition, explosives, radioactive materials, bulk asbestos, or shock-sensitive materials?

    Not HHW

    HHW refuse list includes ammunition; asbestos; explosives; kepones; shock-sensitive materials; radioactive materials. For radioactive materials the How Do I page points to Maryland Department of the Environment, Office of Radiological Health, 410-537-3193. Note: drywall mud or tile mastic containing asbestos is listed as accepted HHW; bulk asbestos is refused — cite How Do I, do not invent a bulk path.

  9. 9

    Thinking Poolesville Beauty Spot for chemicals?

    Wrong path · bulk trash only

    Beauty Spot (19200 Jerusalem Road, Poolesville) is Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m., residents, bulk trash up to 499 lb, no charge. It does not accept household hazardous waste. Bring chemicals to Shady Grove HHW instead.

  10. 10

    Usable leftover paint or stain someone else could use?

    Paint Giveaway · same compound

    HHW facility page: usable paint and stain offered first-come, first-served at the Hazardous Waste Compound. Colors not guaranteed. Use at your own risk; recipients must sign a waiver. Details and latex vs oil rules: leftover-paint page on this site.

Household Hazardous Waste compound · Recycling Area, Shady Grove
Address Household Hazardous Waste Compound (high blue chain-link fence, large roof), Recycling Area, Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station. Last-checked 2 Sep 2026.

ECOWISE · fee-based business / SQG drop-off
Address Same Household Hazardous Waste compound, Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station, 16101 Frederick Road, Derwood, MD 20855. Enter via Frederick Road (MD-355). Last-checked 2 Sep 2026.
Who Montgomery County businesses / Small Quantity Generators generating less than 220 pounds (100 kg) of hazardous waste per calendar month and less than 2.2 pounds (1 kg) of acute hazardous waste per month. Registration is free. No minimum quantity. No contractual obligation. Businesses above those limits: not ECOWISE — destination not stated; call 311. Last-checked 2 Sep 2026.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency: Maryland driver’s license or MD photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • If over 15 gallons liquid or 75 pounds solid: call 311 (or 240-777-0311) to arrange drop-off before you leave home.
  • Containers labeled when you can; unknowns (no identification) are listed as accepted on How Do I — still keep them sealed and upright.
  • Business / SQG: ECOWISE registration packet completed first; payment ready (credit card or business check to Clean Harbors — no cash).
  • Covered load if you self-haul (hours page). PPE (high-vis vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes) is required on the main tipping floor; HHW is the Recycling Area compound, not the tipping floor.

Not here

  • Televisions; computers and related electronics (electronics pad — not HHW).
  • Compressed gas cylinders: acetylene, carbon dioxide, oxygen, propane tanks (upper-lot attendant — not HHW).
  • Medical sharps and syringes; commercial, industrial, or medical wastes (see official county How Do I — this page does not give medical disposal advice).
  • Ammunition; explosives; kepones; shock-sensitive materials; radioactive materials (radioactive: MDE Office of Radiological Health 410-537-3193 per How Do I).
  • Bulk asbestos (How Do I refuses asbestos; drywall mud / tile mastic containing asbestos is listed as accepted — different from bulk asbestos).
  • ECOWISE refuse: explosives; asbestos; radioactive materials; medical waste.
  • Poolesville Beauty Spot for any household hazardous waste.
  • Empty aerosol / empty spray-paint cans as “HHW” — How Do I: discard empty cans as regular trash; cans with remaining product go to HHW.
  • A made-up Sunday winner between Frederick Road and Shady Grove Road gates — unreconciled; confirm the morning you go.
  • Medication disposal instructions on this unofficial page — use official county medicine-collection / safe-disposal pages if that is your item.
