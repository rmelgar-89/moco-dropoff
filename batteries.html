Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Home batteries — alkaline / dry-cell, lithium, lithium-ion, lithium-polymer, Ni-Cd, NiMH (tool packs), button cells, UPS, cordless-tool packs?

    HHW compound · Recycling Area

    Household Hazardous Waste drop-off at Shady Grove. How Do I: accepts all batteries, including alkaline. Residents: no charge. No quantity limit. Enter from Route 355 / Frederick Road; Recycling Area; blue chain-link HHW compound. Proof of residency. Tape or bag terminals before you go. Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only. Separate HHW-only clock: not stated beyond “Transfer Station hours of operation.”

  2. 2

    Lead-acid / car / vehicle battery from home?

    HHW · residents · no charge

    Same HHW compound. How Do I: lead acid or vehicle batteries from residents at no charge. Business path is ECOWISE (choice 5), not free resident HHW. Fees page also lists rechargeable / lead-acid / other non-alkaline as residents-only at the Recyclable Materials Drop-Off Area.

  3. 3

    Single-family / townhome with county-provided recycling — want curb pickup?

    On-call curbside · schedule 311

    Schedule household alkaline and rechargeable batteries with electronic items and scrap metal recycling via MC311 (311 or 240-777-0311) or online (form URL: not stated). Tape ends with clear non-metallic tape; small lidded plastic container (311 also: Ziploc-type bags). Not in blue bin or trash. Set out by 7 a.m. Collection 7 a.m.–9 p.m. on recycling day. Request by 7 p.m. the day before (311). 311 note: batteries and small electronics only collected as part of a larger electronics/scrap pickup — confirm when you schedule. Multi-family / municipal contract: not stated on batteries How Do I as this curbside path.

  4. 4

    Prefer a store drop-off near you?

    Call2Recycle / Battery Network locator

    County How Do I points to the Call2Recycle drop-off locator (Call2Recycle is now The Battery Network — same locator mission). Enter your ZIP; call the retailer before you go. What each store accepts (alkaline vs rechargeable vs e-bike / high-energy): check the locator listing — not scraped here. Earth911 is another public locator if useful. Link out only; do not clone databases on this page.

  5. 5

    Business / organization / Small Quantity Generator?

    ECOWISE · fee · Mon–Fri 8–12

    ECOWISE at the same HHW compound. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–12 p.m. No appointment. Register first. Pay per pound (credit card or business check to Clean Harbors; no cash). Credit-card surcharge not to exceed 3%. Posted examples: alkaline/button $0.63/lb; gel cell $0.63/lb; lithium $6.97/lb; Ni-Cd $2.37/lb. Lead-acid listed as accepted; price line if not on the per-pound table: call 240-777-6587. Or use the retail locator. Over 220 lb hazardous waste / month or over 2.2 lb acute: not ECOWISE — call 311; destination: not stated.

  6. 6

    Thinking trash, blue bin, or the electronics pile for loose batteries?

    Wrong path · fire risk

    How Do I: do not dispose of batteries in regular trash or recycling. Reduce Risk of Trash Fires page names alkaline and rechargeable (lithium, Ni-Cd, lead-acid) batteries as trash fire hazards. Electronics How Do I / campus pad is for electronics with circuit boards — rechargeable / tool / UPS batteries belong at HHW (or curbside with the taped-container rules), not the electronics pile as loose cells.

  7. 7

    Damaged, swollen, leaking, or recalled lithium pack?

    County special path: not stated

    Batteries How Do I does not publish a separate damaged/defective/recalled (DDR) procedure. Do not invent one. Call 311 (or 240-777-0311) before you haul a damaged pack. The Battery Network publishes its own DDR / safety guidance for participating retail sites — that is the locator program’s rule, not a county How Do I line. Confirm before you go.

  8. 8

    Thinking Poolesville Beauty Spot for batteries?

    Wrong path · bulk trash only

    Beauty Spot (19200 Jerusalem Road, Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m.) is bulk trash up to 499 lb for residents. It does not accept household hazardous waste. Batteries belong at Shady Grove HHW (or curbside / retail locator), not Poolesville.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency (residential HHW): Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Tape (clear / masking / other non-metallic) for terminals — or individual plastic bags per battery.
  • Small lidded plastic container (or Ziploc-type bags for curbside) for loose cells. Do not toss loose batteries in a pocket with coins or keys.
  • For curbside: schedule with 311 first; itemized list; set out by 7 a.m. on recycling day.
  • Covered load if you self-haul (hours page). PPE (high-vis vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes) is required on the main tipping floor, not described as required for Recycling Area HHW hand-offs.

Not here

  • Batteries in regular trash (fire risk — How Do I + Reduce Risk of Trash Fires).
  • Batteries in the blue recycling bin.
  • Loose rechargeable / tool / UPS packs on the electronics pad as if they were TVs (wrong pad — use HHW / taped curbside rules).
  • Poolesville Beauty Spot as a battery destination.
  • Scraped Call2Recycle / Battery Network / Earth911 store lists on this page.
  • Business free drop-off of rechargeable / lead-acid / other non-alkaline at the Recyclable Materials Drop-Off Area (fees page: residents only).
  • Invented damaged/defective lithium county procedure (call 311; Battery Network has its own DDR rules for retail sites).
  • Medical sharps / meds pages (YMYL — not this site).
