Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    CFL, fluorescent tube, or ballast from home?

    HHW compound · Recycling Area

    Household Hazardous Waste drop-off at Shady Grove. How Do I lights: accepts CFLs, tubes, and ballasts from your home at no additional charge. No quantity limit at HHW. Enter from Route 355 / Frederick Road; Recycling Area; blue chain-link HHW compound. Proof of residency (HHW How Do I). Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only. Separate HHW-only clock: not stated beyond “Transfer Station hours of operation.”

  2. 2

    LCD or LED bulb from home?

    HHW · residents · no charge

    Same HHW compound. How Do I: LCD or LED bulbs from home → Household Hazardous Waste drop-off at Shady Grove at no charge. Business path is ECOWISE (choice 6), not free resident HHW.

  3. 3

    Incandescent or halogen bulb?

    Regular trash · not HHW

    How Do I: dispose of incandescent and halogen light bulbs in your regular trash collection. Do not haul these to HHW as if they were CFLs.

  4. 4

    Broken CFL or fluorescent tube?

    EPA cleanup · then double-bag trash

    How Do I: CFLs and tubes contain a small amount of mercury; follow US EPA cleanup instructions for broken fluorescent bulbs. Then double-bag the broken CFL or tube in a re-sealable plastic bag and place in household trash. Do not put CFLs or tubes into the recycling bin. Intact unbroken CFLs/tubes still belong at HHW (or a retail drop-off that accepts them) — the trash path above is the county’s broken-bulb disposal line after EPA cleanup. Not medical advice; not a poison-control page.

  5. 5

    Prefer a store drop-off for a few CFLs / tubes?

    County-compiled retail list · small quantities

    How Do I publishes a retail table and says Montgomery County compiled it through various sources: not a complete directory, not an endorsement. Hours of acceptance: vary by location. Cost: no charge (as posted). Community retail: small quantities only. Example cap on the live table: Home Depot #2551 (2300 Broadbirch Dr, Silver Spring) — yes max of 3 bulbs / yes max of 3 tubes. Other rows list bulb-only vs bulb+tube — check the live How Do I before you go; call the store. This unofficial page does not clone that table.

  6. 6

    Business / organization / Small Quantity Generator?

    ECOWISE · fee · Mon–Fri 8–12

    ECOWISE at the same HHW compound. Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–12 p.m. No appointment. Register first. How Do I business line: $1.17 per bulb or 4-foot tube; $2.34 per 8-foot tube; no quantity limit on that line. ECOWISE price table: Fluorescent Tubes 4-ft $1.17 per bulb; 8-ft $2.34 per bulb; Compact Fluorescent Lamp (CFL) bulbs $4.25 per pound (includes container weight). PCB ballasts $2.35/lb on ECOWISE. Pay credit card or business check to Clean Harbors; no cash. Credit-card surcharge not to exceed 3%. Over 220 lb hazardous waste / month or over 2.2 lb acute: not ECOWISE — call 311; destination: not stated.

  7. 7

    Live in a municipality (city / town), not unincorporated county?

    Ask your municipal office · plus HHW still open

    How Do I: contact your local municipal office for additional information regarding CFLs recycling opportunities in your community. County HHW at Shady Grove remains the posted free resident path for home CFLs / tubes / ballasts / LCD·LED. Municipal add-ons: confirm with that office — not inventoried here.

  8. 8

    Thinking blue bin, electronics pad, or Poolesville Beauty Spot for bulbs?

    Wrong path

    How Do I: do not put CFLs or tubes into your recycling bin. Intact mercury lamps belong at HHW (or accepting retail), not the blue bin. Electronics How Do I is for electronics with circuit boards — not a substitute for the lights How Do I. Poolesville Beauty Spot (19200 Jerusalem Road, Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m.) is bulk trash up to 499 lb for residents; it does not accept household hazardous waste.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency (residential HHW): Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Intact CFLs, tubes, ballasts, and/or LCD·LED lamps — keep unbroken lamps whole for HHW or retail drop-off.
  • For a broken CFL/tube: after EPA cleanup, re-sealable plastic bags for double-bagging before household trash.
  • Covered load if you self-haul (hours page). PPE (high-vis vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes) is required on the main tipping floor, not described as required for Recycling Area HHW hand-offs.

Not here

  • Intact CFLs / tubes in the blue recycling bin (How Do I: do not put CFLs or tubes into your recycling bin).
  • Incandescent / halogen hauled to HHW as if they were mercury lamps (How Do I: regular trash).
  • Broken CFL cleanup as medical / poison-control advice on this page (link EPA; not YMYL treatment).
  • Poolesville Beauty Spot as a bulb destination.
  • A scraped or complete retail store directory on this unofficial page (county list is compiled, incomplete, not an endorsement — use the live How Do I).
  • Invented retail hours (county: vary by location).
  • Business free HHW for lamps (ECOWISE fee path).
  • Medical sharps / meds pages (YMYL — not this site).
