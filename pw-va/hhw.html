Which clock do you need?

  1. 1

    Household hazardous waste or electronics recycling?

    HHW · Wed + Sat only

    County Landfill only. Effective 3 Jan 2026: Wednesdays and Saturdays 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Get a ticket from the traffic-control attendant first. Cap: 15 gal or 40 lb HHW and up to 3 electronics per visit. Free for County residents. Not businesses. Not Manassas or Manassas Park.

  2. 2

    Regular trash, recycling, or yard waste (not HHW)?

    Landfill daily · including Sunday

    Landfill & Compost Facility clock (as of 3 Jan 2026): Mon–Wed / Fri–Sun 7 a.m.–4 p.m.; Thursday 7 a.m.–6 p.m. Sunday open for landfill — still not HHW. Motor oil, antifreeze, and household batteries: daily during normal landfill hours (not only HHW days).

  3. 3

    Live in the City of Manassas or Manassas Park?

    HHW refused

    County HHW will not accept HHW from Manassas or Manassas Park residents. Landfill trash/recycling may still be available for a fee for those cities — that is not the HHW program. Confirm city options separately.

  4. 4

    Leftover latex or oil paint?

    HHW accepted list · no PaintCare in VA

    HHW accepted materials include paints (latex & oil), spray paint cans, paint thinners, varnishes, lacquers. Virginia has no PaintCare program — do not send VA paint to MD/DC PaintCare. Latex dry-and-trash: not stated on the HHW page fetched. Treat paint as HHW unless a later official Know Before You Go page says otherwise.

  5. 5

    Thinking Balls Ford compost for HHW?

    Wrong site

    HHW is only at the County Landfill, not the compost facility. Compost does accept used motor oil, antifreeze, and household batteries during normal compost hours.

Bring this

  • Ticket from the traffic-control attendant before using HHW.
  • Original labeled containers, sealed and packaged for safe transport (HHW page).
  • Proof of Prince William County residency (see county proof-of-residency page). Manassas / Manassas Park addresses will not clear HHW.
  • Stay within 15 gal / 40 lb HHW and 3 electronics per visit.

Not here

  • HHW on Sunday (or Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri) — landfill may be open; HHW is not.
  • HHW from Manassas or Manassas Park residents.
  • Business or commercial HHW.
  • HHW at the compost facility.
  • Explosives, ammunition, medical waste, unused medication, biological waste, asbestos, radioactive waste, unknown substances (not accepted at Solid Waste facilities).
  • PaintCare retail drop-off in Virginia — does not exist.
