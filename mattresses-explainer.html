Which path fits?

  1. 1

    Buying a new mattress with delivery in California?

    Retailer take-back · no extra recovery fee

    California law (PRC §42992): the retailer must offer to pick up your used mattress for recovery at no additional cost when the new one is delivered. Delivery / set-up fees for the new product may still apply. Contaminated units that pose a health or safety risk may be refused. Common-carrier / many online deliveries: retailer must offer to arrange pickup within 30 days of delivery. Ask the retailer before delivery day. Sources: CalRecycle mattress EPR; MRC retailer take-back; Bye Bye Mattress California page.

  2. 2

    Live in California, Connecticut, Oregon, or Rhode Island?

    Bye Bye Mattress locator · free drop-off

    Use the Bye Bye Mattress find-a-facility locator (link only — this page does not list sites). Program is for people based in that state; out-of-state units are not in the free program (Bye Bye FAQ). CA & OR: units accepted regardless of condition / purchase date (FAQ). CT & RI: recyclable condition; damaged / soiled / wet / infested — call the site; fees may apply (FAQ). Futon mattress that separates from the frame: accepted in CA only. Crib mattress: accepted in CT only (FAQ). Call ahead for unit limits and appointments.

  3. 3

    Montgomery County, MD (or elsewhere outside MRC states)?

    County rules · not Bye Bye Mattress

    Maryland is not an MRC / Bye Bye Mattress program state. MoCo residents: use the live geo page mocodropoff.com/mattresses (Shady Grove mattress pad, commercial restriction, refuse list) and bulk trash for curbside bulk if you are on county trash. Other counties: contact your local waste authority — do not invent hours or caps here. Bye Bye home page: if the program is not active in your state, ask your city or hauler about bulky items.

  4. 4

    Retailer take-back where you live (non-CA)?

    Ask the seller · rules vary

    A statewide no-cost take-back statute like California’s is not stated here for CT, OR, RI, or Maryland. Some retailers still haul away the old set when they deliver — that is a store policy, not invented on this page. Ask before you buy.

Bring this

  • MRC states: confirm you (and the unit) belong in that state before you drive — out-of-state units are refused from the free program (Bye Bye FAQ).
  • Call the drop-off or event for unit limits, appointments, and whether damaged / wet / infested units are free or fee (especially CT / RI).
  • California delivery: ask the retailer to schedule take-back before delivery day; keep the old mattress accessible and dry if you can.
