Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Fridge, freezer, window AC, dehumidifier, or other refrigerant appliance from home?

    Scrap Metal Drop-off · Recycling Area

    Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station. Hand the unit to a drop-off attendant. County: all refrigerants brought to Scrap Metal Drop-Off are evacuated on site and reused or recycled. How Do I: from your home. Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only. Separate scrap-metal-pad clock: not stated.

  2. 2

    Same items, and you have County-provided recycling and want pickup?

    On-call curbside scrap metal

    Unlimited scrap-metal pickups per calendar year. Schedule ahead. Fridge/freezer: empty all contents; secure the doors shut or remove them. No fee (recycling service details). Set out by 7 a.m. on recycling day. Not the blue bin. Not bulk trash. Municipalities: check the town.

  3. 3

    Other item that is 51% or more metal, larger than a trash can?

    Same scrap-metal program

    Curbside: up to 8 feet long. Transfer Station: any size, including screws, pots, hangers, small household metal. Washer, dryer, stove, dishwasher, microwave: large household appliances on the scrap-metal list. ≥51% metal is scrap, not bulk trash.

  4. 4

    Riding mower or zero-turn?

    Transfer Station only

    Not accepted curbside. Drop off at Shady Grove scrap-metal recycling, drained of oil and gas. No charge for delivering scrap metal items.

  5. 5

    Walk-behind lawn mower, curbside?

    Curbside scrap metal · prepped

    How Do I: empty all fuel and oil; remove batteries if applicable. Pick-up request page also says spark plugs must be removed (How Do I does not). Those two prep lines are unreconciled. Confirm the morning you go.

  6. 6

    Propane, oxygen, or other pressurized tank?

    Not scrap metal

    Refused in all scrap-metal programs. Propane How Do I: hand to an attendant on the upper lot near electronics. Not HHW. Not the blue bin. Not bulk trash. Not this pad.

  7. 7

    Vehicle, or auto parts over 60 lb?

    Not county scrap metal

    How Do I: not accepted in any scrap-metal program. Generally accepted by automobile parts recyclers or wrecking/salvage businesses. Curbside auto parts under 60 lb: metal or steel tire rims only — no engines, mufflers, hoods, fenders, chassis.

  8. 8

    Business / commercial metal appliance?

    Fees page vs “from your home”

    Fees page: metal appliances and other large scrap metal accepted from residents and non-residential/commercial at recyclable drop-off, no charge except as noted. Refrigerants How Do I: “from your home.” Cite both. Do not assume commercial Freon is free beyond that fees-page line.

  9. 9

    Thinking HHW for a fridge or AC?

    Wrong pad

    Refrigerants How Do I sends home units to Scrap Metal Drop-off. HHW accepted list does not include refrigerators, freezers, or air conditioners. HHW refuse list does not name Freon appliances. Same campus, Recycling Area — different pad.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency (residential drop-off): Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Drop-off: hand the refrigerant appliance to a drop-off attendant in the Recycling drop-off area. You do not evacuate Freon yourself. Drop-off empty-and-doors rule: not stated on the refrigerants How Do I page (that prep is posted for curbside).
  • Curbside fridge/freezer: empty all contents; secure the doors shut or remove them. Itemized list of large metal items for 311 / the request form.
  • Walk-behind mower curbside: empty fuel and oil; remove batteries if applicable. Spark plugs: pick-request page says remove them; How Do I does not. Unreconciled.
  • Riding / zero-turn mower: Transfer Station only; drained of oil and gas.
  • Covered load if you self-haul (hours page). PPE (high-vis vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes) is required on the main tipping floor, not described as required for the Recycling Area scrap-metal pad.

Not here

  • HHW compound for a fridge, freezer, or AC (refrigerants How Do I → scrap metal). HHW refuse list does not name Freon appliances; accepted list does not include them either.
  • Poolesville Beauty Spot — does not accept scrap metal.
  • Bulk trash for items 51% or more metal that will not fit in a trash bag or can (bulk How Do I sends those to scrap metal).
  • Oxygen or propane tanks, or other containers under pressure — all scrap-metal programs refuse them. Propane How Do I: upper lot near electronics, hand to attendant.
  • Vehicles or automotive parts weighing more than 60 pounds. Large auto parts also cannot be disposed of at the Transfer Station (bulk How Do I).
  • Latex or oil-based paint cans (scrap-metal refuse). Paint is a different page.
  • TVs and computers — electronics pad, not scrap metal. HHW will not take TVs or computers.
  • Scrap metal in the blue bin.
  • Retailer haul-away (Best Buy and similar) is not a county program on this page. Not invented here.

What they take (scrap-metal How Do I lists)

Copied from the official How Do I page. Rule of thumb: 51% or more metal. If an item is not on the list, How Do I says call 311.

  • Curbside large household appliances: air conditioners (including air-handling units and compressors), clothes dryers, clothes washers, dehumidifiers, dishwashers, freezers, microwave ovens, refrigerators, stoves, wall ovens.
  • Also curbside: bicycles; metal cabinets; metal doors; exercise machines (treadmills, weight benches); chain-link and wire fencing (rolled); metal file cabinets; grills; iron furniture and railings; lawnmowers (prepped); metal sheds (disassembled); shower stalls; metal lawn chairs; sewing machines; metal swing sets (disassembled, concrete off legs); automotive parts under 60 lb — metal or steel tire rims only.
  • Transfer Station extra: all of the above; metal items of any size (screws, nails, hardware, pots and pans, wire clothes hangers, other small household metal); riding mowers for residential yards and zero-turn mowers, drained of oil and gas; items oversized for curbside.

Dehumidifiers are on this scrap-metal large-appliance list. They also appear on the electronics How Do I accept list. This page does not pick a pad for a dehumidifier. Confirm the morning you go. Last-checked 31 Aug 2026.

County line on refrigerants: “all refrigerants brought to the Scrap Metal Drop-Off area at the Transfer Station are evacuated from the appliance and either reused or recycled according to applicable environmental standards.” Last-checked 31 Aug 2026.
