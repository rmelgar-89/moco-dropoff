Which path fits?

  1. 1

    You know the material and can drive it?

    Free · county drop-off first

    Start on the kind page, not a haul quote: leftover paint, bulk trash, mattresses, electronics, freon appliances, HHW. Each page has hours / who / fee / cap / phone from official sources. Self-unload at Shady Grove when the material page says so.

  2. 2

    County-provided trash and bulky furniture / mattress / non-metal junk?

    Free · bulk pickup (5/year)

    Eligible residential properties: up to 5 free bulk collections per calendar year. Schedule ahead (online by 11:59 p.m. the day before trash day, or 311 / 240-777-0311 by 7 p.m. the business day before). Set out by 7 a.m. Details and refuse list: bulk trash. Last-checked 15 Sep 2026 on this summary; confirm on the county page.

  3. 3

    Private / HOA trash, or bulk already used up?

    Self-haul · or your collector

    Private/HOA subscription trash: not eligible for county bulk. Call your collector, or self-haul. Residents: trash under 500 lb at no charge to Shady Grove Transfer Station or Poolesville Beauty Spot (Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m., 19200 Jerusalem Rd — bulk ≤499 lb; not HHW). Proof of residency. See bulk trash and hours.

  4. 4

    Latex paint you can dry at home?

    DIY · dry then trash

    County How Do I: harden or dry residual latex before trash (air-dry if <1/4 full; hardener / kitty litter / mulch / shredded paper if fuller). Wet latex or oil-based → HHW / PaintCare paths on leftover paint. Supplies how-to: paint hardener.

    Drop-off still too far, no vehicle, or cannot unload?
