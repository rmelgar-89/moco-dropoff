Empty — or product left?

  1. 1

    Spray / aerosol can still has product left (from your home)?

    HHW · Shady Grove

    Oil How Do I: if they still contain product and are from your home, bring them to the Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station as household hazardous waste. HHW How Do I lists “spray paint cans with remaining paint” and “aerosol cans with remaining hazardous products” the same way. Residents, proof of residency. No curbside HHW pickup. Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only.

  2. 2

    Spray / aerosol can is empty?

    Regular household trash

    Oil How Do I: if the cans are empty, put them into your regular trash. HHW How Do I: discard empty cans as regular trash. Do not bring empty spray cans to HHW as “hazardous waste.”

  3. 3

    Trying PaintCare for spray paint?

    Not PaintCare · aerosols refused

    PaintCare products we accept: sites do not accept aerosol coatings. PaintCare is for architectural coatings that carry the PaintCare fee (house paint, primer, stain, sealer, shellac, varnish). Use the PaintCare drop-off site locator only for those — this page does not list stores. Empty PaintCare cans are also refused at PaintCare sites.

  4. 4

    Looks commercial, or a large HHW load?

    ECOWISE / 311 · call ahead

    Oil How Do I / HHW: attendants may refuse commercial-looking loads. Contractors (or people believed to be contractors) register for ECOWISE and pay for paint disposal Wednesday 1–5 p.m. (HHW / Paint Giveaway page). Over 15 gallons of liquid or 75 pounds of solid HHW: call 311 (or 240-777-0311) before you go (HHW How Do I).

  5. 5

    Liquid leftover — pour it out?

    No · do not pour

    Do not pour remaining product down a drain, storm sewer, or into household trash as liquid. Empty → trash. Product left → HHW.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency for Shady Grove residential drop-off: Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Cans with product left: keep upright / secured for transport; oil How Do I pattern for wet paint is sealed, leak-proof container or bag and a ventilated vehicle — apply common sense for pressurized cans; do not invent a depressurize-at-home recipe here.
  • If over 15 gallons liquid or 75 pounds solid HHW: call 311 (or 240-777-0311) before you leave home.
  • Empty cans only if truly empty — those go in regular trash, not HHW.
