Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    TV, computer, monitor, phone, printer, stereo, game console, cables, vacuum, or other listed electronics?

    Electronics pad · Recycling Area

    Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station. Residents and businesses. You unload. Trucks you own, borrow, or rent are OK. How Do I: open daily. Facility clock: Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. commercial entrance only. Separate electronics-pad clock: not stated.

  2. 2

    Single-family or townhome with County-provided recycling, and you want pickup?

    On-call curbside

    Schedule electronics and household alkaline/rechargeable batteries with 311 (How Do I also says “or online”; form URL: not stated). Do not put electronics or batteries in the blue bin or trash (fire risk). Small items in a box labeled “Electronics.” Set out by 7 a.m. Collection 7 a.m.–9 p.m. on recycling day. Request by 7 p.m. the day before recycling pickup.

  3. 3

    Rechargeable, tool, or UPS batteries?

    HHW · not the electronics pile

    Take rechargeable batteries, uninterruptible power supplies, and tool batteries to Household Hazardous Waste in the same Recycling Area — or schedule them with a curbside electronics pickup. Not the electronics pad.

  4. 4

    Small household appliance that is mostly metal?

    Scrap metal

    The electronics How Do I page sends small household appliances to scrap metal, not electronics. Freon appliances (fridge, freezer, AC) are not this page.

  5. 5

    Working computer you want reused?

    Donate · wipe first

    County How Do I points to a reuse PDF (PDF URL: not stated on the fetch) and the Volunteer Center donation list. Wipe data before you give it away. Still an option to recycle at the electronics pad.

  6. 6

    Thinking HHW for a TV or computer?

    Wrong pad

    HHW will not take computers and related electronics, or televisions. Different pad. Same campus, Recycling Area.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency (residential): Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • You unload. County: residents are responsible for off-loading from their vehicles.
  • Wipe personal data before drop-off. County requires contractors/nonprofits to erase data on reuse and destroy remaining memory; still wipe. Apple: remove Apple ID and factory reset.
  • Curbside only: box labeled “Electronics” for small items; batteries taped (non-metallic tape) in a lidded plastic container or Ziploc. Itemized list for 311.

Not here

  • TVs or computers at HHW (HHW refuse list).
  • Electronics or batteries in the blue bin or household trash (fire risk).
  • Rechargeable batteries, UPS units, and tool batteries on the electronics pile — HHW (or curbside with electronics).
  • Small household appliances that are mostly metal — scrap metal, per the electronics How Do I page. (Dehumidifiers and water coolers also appear on the electronics accept list; this page does not pick a pad. Confirm the morning you go.)
  • Freon appliances (fridge, freezer, AC) — not this page. Next queue item.
  • Community-based drop-off event dates for 2026: not stated on the electronics How Do I page. MDE eCycling event table fetched today: no events reported to the Department.

What they take (electronics How Do I list)

Copied from the official How Do I page. Do not treat this as a store list. Empty ink/toner only where the county says empty.

  • Computers and related: computer disks; monitors (CRTs and flat screen); mice and keyboards; CPUs; docking stations; flash drives; floppy disks; hard drives; laptops; mainframes; modems; network hubs; peripherals; servers; tablets; terminals; thumb drives.
  • Televisions.
  • Phones: cell and landline. Answering machines.
  • Printers and printer drums; copiers; fax machines; scanners; printers’ empty ink; empty toner cartridges.
  • Audio/video: stereos; radios; speakers; headphones and wireless/Bluetooth; cassette / CD / MP3 / record players; recorders; VCR/DVR/DVD or Blu-ray players, discs, and CDs; VHS tapes; audio tapes; cassette tapes; video cameras; digital cameras and memory cards; cable/satellite boxes; projectors; remote controls.
  • Game consoles and controllers; electronic games and toys; drones.
  • Cables and cords (audio, computer, networking, power); copper / phone / coaxial / computer wire; switching boxes; chips and cards; circuit boards.
  • Vacuums (including small handheld); fans; air fryers; air purifiers; room heaters; dehumidifiers; water coolers; bug zappers; flashlights (no bulbs); small handheld electric tools; holiday string lights; solar light fixtures; electronic power generators (solar-panel electronic accessories and light fixtures).
  • Also posted: activity/fitness trackers; alarm clocks; clocks; calculators; GPS; electronic smartwatches; electronic reading devices; portable chargers; power supplies; typewriters; home security systems; surveillance cameras; digital home thermostats; diagnostic/sensing/control equipment; radar detectors.

County line: “All collected items are recycled.” Last-checked 30 Aug 2026.
