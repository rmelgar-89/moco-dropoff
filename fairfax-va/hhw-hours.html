Fairfax County HHW hours: I-66 vs I-95 (which site is open today)

Unofficial. Not Fairfax County. Not a hauler. The trap: I-66 is closed Wednesday; I-95 HHW is only Wednesday and Saturday; Tuesday/Thursday at I-66 starts at noon; Sunday HHW is I-66 only. Last checked 15 Sep 2026

Hours change. Confirm on the official locations-and-hours page the morning you go. This page is unofficial and not the county. Not a hauler. Holiday names and holiday clock hours: not stated. No after-hours drop-off.

Which day is it?

  1. 1

    Monday?

    I-66 HHW · 7 a.m.–5 p.m.

    I-95 HHW closed. I-66 open. Trash/recycling at both sites has its own residential clock (I-66 and I-95 Mon–Fri 7–5 for trash/recycling).

  2. 2

    Tuesday or Thursday?

    I-66 HHW · 12 p.m.–5 p.m. only

    Do not arrive at 7 a.m. for HHW — HHW/e-waste opens at noon. I-95 HHW closed those days.

  3. 3

    Wednesday?

    I-95 HHW · 7 a.m.–5 p.m.

    I-66 HHW is closed Wednesday. Use I-95 Landfill Complex for HHW/e-waste.

  4. 4

    Friday?

    I-66 HHW · 7 a.m.–5 p.m.

    I-95 HHW closed. I-66 open.

  5. 5

    Saturday?

    Both · 7 a.m.–4 p.m.

    I-66 and I-95 HHW/e-waste both open 7 a.m.–4 p.m.

  6. 6

    Sunday?

    I-66 HHW only · 7 a.m.–4 p.m.

    I-95 HHW is closed Sunday. Trash/recycling at I-95 may still be open 7–4 — that is not HHW. Latex-only Lot B at I-95 is a separate paint stream (see leftover-paint page).

Bring this

  • Proof of Fairfax County residency (may be required): driver’s license, state ID, property tax bill, or utility bill.
  • For HHW loads: original containers, lids tight, upright in a cardboard box (HHW packing rules on the HHW page).
  • For e-cycling: delete personal data; remove batteries that need separate HHW recycling when the electronics page says so; stay within 10 large items.

Not here

  • I-66 on Wednesday for HHW.
  • I-95 on Sunday for HHW.
  • After-hours drop-off.
