Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Latex / water-based, more than ¼ can full?

    Recycle at I-66 or I-95 · facility open days

    County HHW PDF: recycle latex in quantities greater than ¼ of a can at I-95 or I-66 any day of the week the facility is open. At I-95, the HHW page also lists a Latex Paint ONLY Drop-off — Lot B on trash/recycling hours (Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–5 p.m.; Sat & Sun 7 a.m.–4 p.m.). Confirm Lot B signs the morning you go.

  2. 2

    Latex / water-based, less than ¼ can?

    Dry · then trash

    Leave the can open to dry. Once dry, bag with household trash on your normal collection day. HHW page tip: pour onto newspaper, dry, roll up, trash. Last resort: bring wet latex to HHW at I-66 or I-95.

  3. 3

    Oil-based paint, stain, varnish, thinner, solvent?

    Attended HHW

    I-66 or I-95 Household Hazardous Waste during HHW hours (not the same as every trash/recycling day). Paint, thinners, stains, varnish listed on the HHW accept list. Free for Fairfax County residents.

  4. 4

    Usable latex (~90% full)?

    Habitat via HHW

    County HHW PDF: usable paint (cans 90% full) may be donated to Habitat for Humanity through HHW’s program. Confirm with attendants the morning you go.

  5. 5

    Thinking PaintCare (Maryland store)?

    Wrong state

    Virginia is not a PaintCare program state. Do not take Fairfax cans to Maryland PaintCare drop-offs. Use county I-66 / I-95 rules above. Locator may show non-program flags for VA — still not a store list to copy.

  6. 6

    Business / commercial paint?

    Not residential HHW

    Business hazardous waste is regulated separately. County points to Commercial Hazardous Waste / Very Small Quantity Generator events — not this residential page.

Bring this

  • Proof of Fairfax County residency (may be required): driver’s license, state ID, property tax bill, or utility bill.
  • Original containers, lids tight; do not mix products. Leaking cans: larger plastic container with tight lid. Secure upright in a cardboard box — not plastic bags (HHW packing page).
  • Transport in trunk or truck bed; ventilate the cabin if needed. Keep flammables out of heat/sun.

Not here

  • PaintCare retail drop-off in Virginia (no program).
  • Maryland PaintCare stores for Fairfax cans.
  • Business / commercial hazardous waste at residential HHW.
  • After-hours drop-off (electronics page: not permitted).
  • Medicines / sharps: county HHW page has separate trash guidance — not this paint page (no YMYL how-to here).
