Where to take leftover paint in Montgomery County, MD

Latex vs oil, hours, ID, 5-gallon cap. Last checked 29 Aug 2026.

Hours and caps change. Confirm on the official page the morning you go. Do not pour paint down a drain, storm sewer, or as liquid in the trash.

Which kind is it?

  1. Still usable, not frozen — Shady Grove Paint Giveaway (waiver, first-come) or a neighbor list.
  2. Latex / water-based leftover — dry it at home, then trash; or drop wet at HHW / a PaintCare site (5 gal/month wet at HHW).
  3. Oil-based, stain, solvent — HHW at Shady Grove (no extra charge for residents) or PaintCare if it is an architectural coating.
  4. Spray cans with product left — HHW. Empty spray cans go in the trash.
  5. Looks commercial, or more than 5 gal wet this month — ECOWISE (contractors: Wednesday 1–5 p.m., they pay) or call 311. Over 15 gal liquid / 75 lb solid: call 311 first.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency: MD license/ID with a Montgomery County address, a current utility bill, or a mortgage/lease. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Rules.
  • Sealed, leak-proof container or bag for wet paint. Ventilate the car.
  • Tipping floor (not the HHW compound) needs a high-vis vest, hard hat, and closed-toe shoes.

Dry latex at home

Under 1/4 full: lid off, ventilated, a few days. Fuller: paint hardener (or kitty litter, mulch, shredded paper), mix, wait about 30 minutes until it will not spill. Lids off, bag, trash. County collection: max 4 cans per set-out.
