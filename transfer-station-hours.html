Where to take trash and recycling at the Montgomery County transfer station (hours, which gate, Sunday)

Two Shady Grove gates, Sunday rule, Poolesville Saturday. Last checked 30 Aug 2026

Hours, fees, and gates change. Confirm on the official page the morning you go. This page is unofficial and not the county. Not a hauler. Holiday names and holiday clock hours: not stated.

Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Sunday?

    Commercial and Truck entrance

    16055 Shady Grove Road, Gaithersburg, MD 20877. 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Residents only. No commercial vehicles, no businesses, no commercial waste, no commercial or rental vehicles, no loads over 500 lb. Scales are closed; if trash is not clearly under 500 lb, the county says return Monday. Residential entrance (16101 Frederick Road) is closed Sundays (and holidays / when the Scale House is closed to commercial traffic). Holiday clock hours: not stated.

  2. 2

    Monday–Saturday, residential, recyclables and/or trash that appears under 500 lb?

    Residential entrance

    16101 Frederick Road, Derwood, MD 20855. 7 a.m.–5 p.m. Proof of Montgomery County residency. No out-of-county waste. Trailer max 5 ft × 9 ft, 24-inch sideboards. No trailers at the Public Unloading Facility (PUF).

  3. 3

    Monday–Saturday, ≥500 lb trash, commercial/truck, or trailer larger than 5 ft × 9 ft?

    Commercial and Truck entrance

    16055 Shady Grove Road, Gaithersburg, MD 20877. 7 a.m.–5 p.m. Weigh at the scales and pay the posted fee. In-county material only. Contractors must verify job location / county of origin or they will not be permitted to dump.

  4. 4

    Tipping floor (main floor)?

    PPE required

    High-visibility vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes. Without PPE, the county says exit and return with it. PUF PPE: not stated. Do not invent it.

  5. 5

    Poolesville instead?

    Beauty Spot

    Saturday 8 a.m.–4 p.m., 19200 Jerusalem Road, Poolesville, MD 20837. Residents only. Bulk trash under 500 lb / up to 499 lb. No charge. Not a full transfer station. Not HHW, not scrap, not yard trim.

Do not copy gate hours onto the HHW compound. Commercial mattresses and box springs: county restriction effective 1 July 2026 (commercial loads need a residential manifest for same-day collection within Montgomery County, or they are turned away). See the mattresses page when it is live. Not this page’s decision tree.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency (residential): Maryland license or photo ID with a Montgomery County address; a current utility bill with a physical MoCo address and your name; or a mortgage statement / lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Covered load. County code requires it (residents, self-haulers, haulers). Uncovered: verbal warning, written Notice of Violation, or a citation. Tarping for Haulers.
  • PPE if you go onto the main tipping floor: high-visibility vest, hard hat, closed-toe shoes. PUF PPE: not stated.
  • Contractors: verification of job location / county of origin. In-county material only.

Not here

  • Uncovered loads (warning / notice / citation).
  • Out-of-county waste.
  • Sunday: commercial vehicles, businesses, commercial waste, commercial or rental vehicles, loads over 500 lb.
  • Shingles or roofing materials of any kind.
  • Car parts (disposal or recycling).
  • Paper shredding at the transfer station (the hours page still says they do not shred).
