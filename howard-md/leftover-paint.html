Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Wet paint (latex or oil) on a Saturday, April–November?

    Alpha Ridge HHW · 8 a.m.–4 p.m.

    Residents’ Convenience Center, 2350 Marriottsville Rd. HHW accepts paint and paint thinners. Proof of Howard County residency (driver’s license). Residential only. Chemists at the analyzing tables.

  2. 2

    Latex you can dry at home?

    Solidify · then trash

    County landfill guide tip (research brief): air-dry or solidify latex with kitty litter, shredded newspaper, or paint hardener, then regular trash. Guide PDF fetch redirected/blocked today — treat as guide-sourced; confirm on Know Before You Throw / guide before relying on it.

  3. 3

    Architectural leftover paint any day of the week?

    PaintCare Maryland · locator

    Statewide program began April 2026. Free drop-off of PaintCare products (house paint, primer, stain, sealer, varnish that carried the fee). Typical site cap 5 gal/visit — call the site. Locator only — this page does not list stores. Aerosols / unlabeled / leaking: not PaintCare.

  4. 4

    Thinking weekday HHW because the dump is open?

    Wrong clock

    Convenience center: Mon–Sat 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (no trailers/box trucks per guide). Transfer station: Mon–Sat 8 a.m.–3 p.m. HHW is Saturdays only, Apr–Nov. Dump open ≠ HHW open.

  5. 5

    December–March?

    HHW seasonal off · PaintCare / dry latex

    HHW page: Saturdays April–November; “typically three special Saturday collections during the winter off-season” — dates: not stated on the page fetched. Prefer PaintCare locator or dry latex for trash until you confirm a winter Saturday.

Bring this

  • Howard County driver’s license (proof of residency for HHW).
  • Original containers or secure, clearly labeled, leak-proof containers; lids tight; do not mix unknowns.
  • If a container leaks: pack in a larger leak-proof package with absorbent (cat litter).
  • Group similar materials for unloading.

Not here

  • Weekday HHW at Alpha Ridge (Saturdays Apr–Nov only).
  • Commercial, industrial, or medical waste at resident HHW.
  • Ammunition, asbestos, explosives, fireworks, radioactive materials (refuse list / other agencies).
  • A scraped list of PaintCare store addresses.
