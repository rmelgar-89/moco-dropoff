Which path?

  1. 1

    Leftover house paint, primer, stain, or varnish (any day)?

    PaintCare DC · locator

    Zero Waste: “the most convenient option.” Separate DC program (since Nov 2016) — not Maryland PaintCare. Free drop-off for PaintCare products. Use the locator; this page does not list stores. Hotline 855-724-6809. Limited-time door-to-door for households in wards 2, 4, 7, 8 with ~10+ gallons: paintcare.org/dc-d2d.

  2. 2

    Also have other HHW, or prefer a DPW event for paint?

    Special Waste · Eventbrite

    Pre-register. One 30-minute arrival window per vehicle (not service time). Bring proof of DC residency + ticket. Latex and oil-based paints are on the accepted list (latex note points to PaintCare). No entry after 2:00 p.m. Capacity or weather can close the gate — check @DCDPW / @dczerowaste.

  3. 3

    Thinking Fort Totten Transfer Station for paint or HHW?

    Wrong building

    Fort Totten unacceptable list includes Household Hazardous Waste and e-cycling (computers/TVs/electronics taken at HHW events). Up to four tires and some appliances are different streams — not paint. Do not treat Fort Totten as the paint drop.

  4. 4

    Commercial waste or no Eventbrite ticket?

    Not the DPW resident event

    Events: DC residents only; no commercial waste. Registration required. Walk-up without registration: not stated as allowed on the pages fetched.

Bring this

  • PaintCare path: sealed PaintCare-product containers; call the site about caps.
  • DPW event path: Eventbrite ticket for your 30-minute arrival window; proof of DC residency (DL, utility bill, or lease); arrive within the window; no entry after 2:00 p.m.
  • One ticket per vehicle; free registration.

Not here

  • Paint or HHW at Fort Totten Transfer Station.
  • Walk-up to a Special Waste event without Eventbrite (not stated as allowed).
  • Commercial waste at DPW Special Waste events.
  • A scraped PaintCare store-address list on this page.
  • Assuming Maryland PaintCare fees/sites apply — DC is a separate program.
  • Stale Fort Totten copy that still mentions old RFK Lot 3 HHW through Mar 2024 — events now use listed lots (e.g. Lot 8) via Eventbrite.
