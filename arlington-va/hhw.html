Hours, appointments, and closures change. Confirm on the official page the morning you go. This page is unofficial and not the county. Not a hauler.

Which path?

  1. 1

    Going Monday–Thursday?

    Appointment required

    Call 703-228-5000 during weekday business hours (7 a.m.–7 p.m. Monday–Friday) to schedule. Appointment drop-off clock on the live HHM page beyond “by appointment”: not stated. Confirm when you book.

  2. 2

    Going Saturday?

    Walk-in 9 a.m.–3 p.m.

    No appointment necessary. Closed Saturdays closest to / actual holidays of Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year’s Day (see County Holiday Schedule). Also closed during spring/fall E-CARE.

  3. 3

    Latex paint less than 25% full?

    Dry at home · trash

    Add kitty litter to a thick paste; bag; curbside trash. More than 25% full → HHM. Oil-based: no curbside; HHM only. No PaintCare in Virginia.

  4. 4

    Thinking Earth Products Yard (4300 29th St. S.)?

    Wrong site for HHM

    Earth Products Yard is inert/metal — not Household Hazardous Materials. HHM is at 565 31st St. S. (as of Oct 2025).

  5. 5

    Business or commercial hazardous materials?

    Not accepted

    HHM is for Arlington County residents and employees only. No charge for that service. Commercial HHM: not accepted.

Arlington HHM Facility
Address 565 31st St. S., Arlington, VA — surface lot across from the Water Pollution Control Plant (as of October 2025). Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.
Who Arlington County residents and employees. No charge. Business and commercial hazardous materials not accepted. Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.
What they take Most solid, liquid, compressed-gas, and aerosol products used in the home (paint, motor oil, pesticides, fertilizer, fluorescent tubes, etc.). Latex >25% full; oil-based paint. Unaccepted: ammunition/explosives, friable asbestos, medical wastes, prescription medications. E-CARE events (biannual) also take paint products with a 25-can limit (separate event — confirm dates on county pages). Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.
Fee No charge for residents/employees. Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.
Cap Year-round HHM facility numeric cap: not stated on the HHM page fetched. E-CARE paint products: 25-can limit (event page). Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.

Virginia has no PaintCare program. Do not send Arlington paint to Maryland or DC PaintCare sites as a substitute for county rules. Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.

Bring this

  • Appointment confirmation if Mon–Thu (call 703-228-5000, Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–7 p.m. to schedule).
  • ID for E-CARE events (county: bring ID). Year-round HHM proof detail beyond residents/employees: not stated on the HHM block fetched.
  • Latex <25%: kitty litter, gloves, eye protection, well-ventilated area if drying at home.
  • Original containers when possible; products marked danger / warning / caution.

Not here

  • Walk-in on a weekday without an appointment.
  • Earth Products Yard (4300 29th St. S.) for HHM.
  • Old pre–Oct 2025 HHM address assumptions — use 565 31st St. S.
  • Business / commercial hazardous materials.
  • Ammunition, explosives, friable asbestos, medical wastes, prescription medications.
  • PaintCare retail drop-off in Virginia — does not exist.
  • Oil-based paint in curbside trash.
