Which path fits?

  1. 1

    Still usable latex or oil paint (not frozen)?

    Free · giveaway / neighbor

    County How Do I: bring usable, not-frozen paint to Shady Grove HHW for the Paint Giveaway (first-come; waiver; colors not guaranteed), or offer it on Buy Nothing / Freecycle / similar. Wet HHW drop still capped. See leftover paint.

  2. 2

    Wet leftover — do not want to dry it?

    Free · HHW or PaintCare

    Skip buying hardener. Drop wet latex (and home oil-based) at Shady Grove HHW — up to 5 gallons wet paint per calendar month, MoCo residents, proof of residency. Architectural coatings that carried the PaintCare fee may use a PaintCare locator site (link only — no store scrape). Details: leftover paint, HHW, latex vs oil.

  3. 3

    Latex / water-based, can <1/4 full?

    DIY free · air-dry then trash

    County How Do I: lid off, safe ventilated area, few days until residual paint is hard/dry. Then lids off; bag cans with household trash (bags in rigid trash cans). No commercial hardener required for this case.

  4. 4

    Latex / water-based, more than 1/4 full — absorb at home?

    DIY free · kitty litter / mulch / paper

    County How Do I allows mulch, kitty litter, or shredded paper (takes longer than packet hardener). Stir thoroughly; wait until tacky / oatmeal-like and will not spill. Wet or soft paint that could spill is left as unacceptable by collection crews. Then lids off; trash with household set-out rules below.

  5. 5

    Same fuller latex can — prefer commercial waste paint hardener?

    Supplies · affiliate placeholder

    County How Do I: waste paint hardener (one packet generally treats up to one gallon); mix thoroughly; ~30 minutes until it will not spill. Optional bags for set-out. Buy only if you want this path — free absorbents and wet drop-off still work. Sponsored placeholder link below (disclosure next to it).

  6. 6

    Oil-based paint, stain, or aerosols with product left?

    Not trash dry-out · HHW

    Do not treat this page as an oil-based dry-out guide. Home oil-based → HHW (and PaintCare if architectural). Empty oil cans → trash. Aerosols with product → HHW; empty aerosols → trash (oil How Do I). See leftover paint / HHW.

Do not pour paint down a drain, storm sewer, or as liquid in household trash. Last-checked 15 Sep 2026.

Bring this / prep

  • Confirm latex vs oil on the can label before you dry anything for trash.
  • Stir absorbent or hardener thoroughly; wait until paint will not spill.
  • Lids off for dried latex set-out; bags inside rigid trash cans (county language).
  • Count cans: max 4 per county-trash set-out under 45 lb each — or schedule bulk / use HHW.
  • Proof of residency if you switch to wet HHW drop-off instead.
