Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    County-provided trash, and you have a sofa / carpet / other non-metal bulk item?

    On-call curbside bulk · 5/year

    Five bulk trash collections per calendar year per residential property with County-provided trash. Schedule ahead. Itemized non-metal list. Online request by 11:59 p.m. the day before your trash day; phone 311 / 240-777-0311 by 7:00 p.m. the business day before. Collection window: 7:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. on that County collection day. Set out by 7 a.m., adjacent to household trash, separate from recycling / yard trim / scrap metal. Disassemble huge items so at least one side is under 4 ft (examples named: upright pianos, hot tubs, backyard play sets). Late request → next week’s trash day.

  2. 2

    Not sure if trash is County or private / HOA?

    Collection Day Lookup · or 311

    Use the county Collection Day Lookup, or call 311 (out-of-County: 240-777-0311). Who-provides page: same-day trash+recycling often means County trash is possible; different-day trash vs recycling, or twice-a-week trash, usually means private subscription — confirm with the lookup, not this unofficial page. Then follow the bulk path for your provider.

  3. 3

    Private / HOA / community-association trash subscription?

    Not county bulk · call your collector

    Bulk pick-up request page: residents with private/subscription trash via an individual or homeowner/community association contract are not eligible for County bulk trash service. How Do I: contact your trash collector; some private collectors may charge additional fees (amounts: not stated). Municipalities: check your town or city. Or self-haul (choice 5).

  4. 4

    Item is 51% or more metal (fridge, grill, bike frame…)?

    Scrap metal · not bulk

    Bulk How Do I refuse: large items ≥51% metal that won’t fit a trash bag/can → request scrap metal recycling, not bulk. County-provided recycling: unlimited scrap-metal pickups per calendar year when scheduled (separate form / 311). Freon appliances → scrap-metal pad / curbside scrap — see Freon appliances. Propane tanks → refused as bulk and as scrap; see tires / propane.

  5. 5

    Self-haul instead of (or because you are not on) county bulk?

    Shady Grove · or Beauty Spot Sat

    County residents: loads of trash under 500 lb at no charge at Shady Grove Processing Facility and Transfer Station or Poolesville Beauty Spot. Beauty Spot: Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m. only, 19200 Jerusalem Road — bulk trash up to 499 lb; not HHW / scrap / yard trim / paper / commingled / regular household trash / commercial waste. Transfer Station also takes trash ≥500 lb — fee schedule ($70/ton trash ≥500 lb on live fees page); Sunday loads >500 lb not accepted. Proof of residency. Covered load at Transfer Station / Beauty Spot page note for Transfer Station loads.

  6. 6

    Mattress or box spring?

    Bulk accept list · details on mattresses page

    Bulk How Do I accepts mattresses and box springs as curbside bulk (County-trash path). Transfer Station also posts mattress recycling / commercial restriction effective 1 Jul 2026 — do not treat this bulk page as the full mattress tree. See mattresses (draft; not live until the owner says).

  7. 7

    5+ bags/cans of regular trash, or one minor remodel piece (door / vanity)?

    Counts as bulk · or small C&D cans

    How Do I: 5 or more bags or cans of trash is a bulk rule-of-thumb. Single minor-repair items (wood door, bathroom vanity) are on the bulk accept list — not major remodel/demolition debris. Building-materials-trash How Do I (separate page): County-trash homes may set out two cans/week of construction debris under 45 lb each without a bulk request, or schedule one bulk item (door/sink/toilet/cabinet) up to five times/year. Major C&D (bricks, concrete, lumber, shingles, drywall, sheds, etc.) is refused as curbside bulk.

  8. 8

    Need to cancel or change a scheduled bulk request?

    Call 311 · not online

    Call 311 or 240-777-0311 by 7:00 p.m. on the business day before collection. Updates and cancellations cannot be done online (bulk pick-up request page).

  9. 9

    Thinking Beauty Spot for HHW, scrap, yard trim, or bulbs?

    Wrong path

    Beauty Spot accepts bulk trash up to 499 lb only. Official refuse: commercial waste; regular household trash; scrap metal; yard trim; mixed paper and cardboard; commingled recycling; household hazardous waste. HHW / bulbs / batteries → Shady Grove paths on those pages — not Poolesville.

Bring this

  • For curbside: itemized list of non-metal bulk items; request confirmation from online form or 311; items disassembled so at least one side is under 4 ft if needed.
  • For self-haul: proof of residency — Maryland license/photo ID with a Montgomery County address; current utility bill with physical MoCo address and your name; or mortgage/lease in your name for a physical MoCo address. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Proof of County Residency.
  • Covered load for Transfer Station (Beauty Spot page also states all loads must be covered when bringing recyclables or trash to the Transfer Station).
  • Rugs/carpet: roll and tie. Mirrors / glass tops: cover with newspaper and tape (uncovered glass refused as curbside bulk).
  • Payment method if your load may hit ≥500 lb at Shady Grove: credit/debit (no cash/checks).

Not here

  • County bulk eligibility for private / HOA subscription trash addresses (official: not eligible).
  • Items ≥51% metal as bulk (scrap metal path).
  • Propane tanks as bulk (refused).
  • County recycling bins/carts as bulk (schedule damaged-bin pickup instead).
  • Uncovered glass mirrors/table tops as curbside bulk.
  • Major construction / remodel / demolition debris as curbside bulk (bricks, concrete, lumber, non-metal fencing, shingles, drywall, paint, rock, sod, auto parts, non-metal kitchen cabinets, sheds, large dog houses, etc.).
  • Grand pianos as curbside bulk (refused). Upright pianos appear only as a disassembly example for oversized items — not a free pass for grands.
  • Hazardous materials; fecal/infectious waste; dead animals as bulk.
  • Beauty Spot as HHW, scrap, yard trim, paper, commingled, or regular household trash.
  • Tree stumps >3 ft diameter or large auto parts at the Transfer Station.
  • Invented private-hauler prices, GOT-JUNK reviews, or dumpster quotes (affiliates not applied on this page).
  • Medical sharps / meds pages (YMYL — not this site).
