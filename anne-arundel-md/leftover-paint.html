Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Latex / water-based leftover?

    Recycling center · solidify first

    Not accepted curbside. Not the HHW Saturday path for disposal of liquid latex as HHW. 2026 brochure: solidify with kitty litter, sawdust, or dirt; seal in original container; drop at any County Recycling Center year-round. FAQs also point to PaintCare for latex and oil house paint up to 5 gal.

  2. 2

    Oil-based paint, stains, solvents?

    HHW Saturday only

    Hazardous. Only on scheduled HHW Saturdays (not recycling centers, not landfill — oil-based paints and liquids are on the centers’ not-accepted list). 2026 dates: 3/21, 4/18, 5/16, 6/6, 9/12, 10/10 — all at Heritage Office Complex, 2662 Riva Rd, Annapolis, 8:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.

  3. 3

    Want a year-round retail drop for house paint?

    PaintCare MD · locator

    Maryland PaintCare (statewide). Free drop-off for qualifying architectural coatings. Use the locator — this page does not list stores. Typical site cap often 5 gal/visit (confirm with the site). Hotline 855-724-6809.

  4. 4

    Driving a rental, dump truck, flatbed, box truck, or rental trailer?

    Refused

    HHW: commercially registered vehicles, including rentals, and commercial waste not accepted. Recycling centers: solid waste in rental / dump / flatbed / stake / box / rental-trailer / double-axle trailer not accepted. Bring a personally registered passenger vehicle with Anne Arundel DL + registration.

  5. 5

    Live in the City of Annapolis?

    Separate program

    County FAQs say Annapolis has its own recycling program. Do not assume County recycling centers or HHW apply. Check annapolis.gov.

Bring this

  • Anne Arundel County driver’s license and vehicle registration.
  • Personally registered vehicle — not a rental or listed commercial body style.
  • Latex: solidified (kitty litter, sawdust, or dirt), sealed in original container.
  • Oil / HHW: original containers when possible; lids tight; boxed or in totes; remain in the car at events.

Not here

  • Liquid latex at the curb.
  • Oil-based paint at recycling centers or the Millersville landfill.
  • Rental vehicles at HHW or (for solid waste) recycling centers.
  • Commercial waste at HHW.
  • City of Annapolis residents assuming County rules apply without checking the city.
  • A scraped PaintCare store-address list on this page.
