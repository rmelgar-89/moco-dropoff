Which kind is it?

  1. Resident, dry, not infested, not a juvenile product — Shady Grove upper-lot mattress recycling pad (across from HHW). License scanned to track count. Numeric cap: not stated.
  2. Curbside bulk trash (County-provided trash service) — mattresses and box springs are listed as accepted bulk items. Five bulk collections per calendar year; schedule ahead. Bulk trash How Do I.
  3. Commercial load (stake body, open-top, etc.), in force since Wednesday 1 July 2026 — accepted only with a residential manifest verifying same-day collection within Montgomery County. No manifest → rejected. All other commercial mattresses turned away; not permitted for disposal on the tipping floor at any time.
  4. Wet / bed bugs / feces / blood / dirt-mud / broken-bent-folded — refuse (contamination list). Do not assume they will take it as trash instead — that is not stated.
  5. Futon, sofa bed, crib or bassinet mattress, water/gel/air bed without upholstery, unattached topper, pillows, sleeping bag, juvenile products — not the mattress program. Futon / sofa bed → bulk trash (non-metal furniture). Toppers: the How Do I A–Z list files “foam rubber cushions and mattress toppers” under trash; the trash How Do I page says curbside trash or Shady Grove, or bulk pickup if too large and you have County trash service.

Bring this

  • Proof of residency for residential users: MD license/ID with a Montgomery County address, a current utility bill, or a mortgage/lease. Out-of-county plates still need one of those. Rules.
  • A driver’s license to scan. The county scans it to track how many mattresses each resident and business brings. Numeric cap: not stated.
  • A dry mattress or box spring from the accept list — not broken, bent, or folded; not stored uncovered / water-saturated; no bed bugs, feces, blood, dirt, or mud.
  • Commercial loads (stake body, open-top, etc.): a residential manifest verifying same-day collection within Montgomery County. No manifest → rejected. Other commercial mattresses are turned away and not permitted on the tipping floor.

They will take

From the official How Do I page (and its program flyer PDF): adjustable-base, hybrid, innerspring, latex, and memory foam mattresses; metal and wood bed-base foundations / box springs. Definition: resilient material enclosed by ticking, intended or promoted for sleeping upon, including renovated foundations and mattresses.

They will not take

  • Car bed; crib or bassinet mattress; fold-out sofa bed or futon; pillows; sleeping bags; unattached pillow top / mattress topper / pad / under pad; upholstered furniture that does not contain a detachable mattress.
  • Juvenile products: carriage, crib bumper, dressing table, infant carrier, lounge pad, pads for juvenile products, playpen, stroller.
  • Liquid- or gaseous-filled ticking, including an adjustable water/gel/air bed without upholstery between ticking and core.
  • Contamination: broken / bent / folded; water-saturated / stored uncovered; bed-bug infestation; feces and blood; dirt and mud. The mattress page does not say these may go to the tipping floor as trash.
