Which kind is it?

  1. 1

    Alkaline / zinc-carbon (AA, AAA, C, D, 9V — single-use)?

    Often trash · or recycle if local / retailer accepts

    EPA Used Household Batteries: in most communities these can go in household trash; EPA still recommends battery recyclers or your local/state solid waste authority. Some jurisdictions refuse trash disposal — Montgomery County, MD does (HHW / county paths on the geo page). Battery Network: primary alkaline accepted at select partners only — check the locator listing. Terminal protection: Battery Network says alkaline usually need none unless marked Lithium.

  2. 2

    Rechargeable — NiMH, Li-ion, Ni-Cd, Ni-Zn, tool packs, small sealed lead-acid (SSLA)?

    Recycle · never trash or blue bin

    EPA: do not put rechargeables in trash or municipal recycling. Removable packs → specialized recyclers, retail take-back, or HHW. Devices with non-removable packs → certified electronics recyclers, retail electronics take-back, or local HHW / electronics programs. Tape terminals or bag each battery. Use The Battery Network locator or Earth911 (link only).

  3. 3

    Button / coin cell or lithium primary (single-use “Lithium” on the label)?

    Recycle · tape or bag · not trash

    EPA: do not put button-cell, coin, or lithium single-use batteries in trash or municipal recycling bins. Bag separately or tape terminals (or wrap the whole button). Look for the word “Lithium” on the cell. Battery Network lists lithium primary and coin/button under primary batteries — select partners; confirm on locator. Keep away from young children (swallowing hazard — EPA).

  4. 4

    Car / boat / wet-cell lead-acid battery?

    Battery retailer or HHW · not trash

    EPA Automotive Batteries: return lead-acid to a battery retailer or local solid / household hazardous waste program; do not put in trash or municipal recycling. Contains sulfuric acid and lead — follow battery warnings. Battery Network consumer collection boxes are for dry-cell / household programs — wet-cell car batteries are a different path (retailer exchange / HHW). MoCo residents: see geo /batteries.

  5. 5

    E-bike, scooter, lawn equipment, or other medium-format / high-energy pack?

    Locator · specialty / brand programs

    Battery Network: medium-format (e-bikes, scooters, outdoor power) and high-energy packs may need specialty partners; e-bike and high-energy programs accept participating brands only. Some locator sites accept e-bike or high-energy (>300 Wh) — check the listing. EV traction packs: certified EV recovery only — not a consumer box. Fees and brand lists: not stated as a national flat number here — confirm at locator / manufacturer.

  6. 6

    Damaged, swollen, leaking, overheating Li-ion — or recalled pack?

    Special handling · not the retail box

    Battery Network: do not put DDR lithium in trash, home recycling, or standard collection boxes. Isolate; place in non-flammable material such as sand or kitty litter per their safety page; contact the manufacturer. Recalled packs: not accepted at Battery Network sites — manufacturer instructions. Locator may show damaged/defective sites in IL, NY, VT, and DC. Corroded alkaline is not “damaged” under their definition. MoCo: county How Do I has no separate DDR procedure — call 311 (geo page).

  7. 7

    Montgomery County, MD resident?

    MoCo geo · not this national dump

    County takes a stricter path than “most communities” alkaline-in-trash. Use live geo mocodropoff.com/batteries for HHW, on-call curbside, ECOWISE, and refuse-from-trash rules. This explainer does not invent MoCo hours or fees.

Bring this / prep

  • Identify chemistry from the label (Li-ion, NiMH, Ni-Cd, Lithium, alkaline) — EPA: type is by marking, not shape or color alone.
  • Bag each rechargeable / lithium / button cell in a clear bag, or tape the positive terminal with clear packing, electrical, or duct tape (Battery Network). Avoid masking / painters / Scotch tape.
  • Soft-sided packs: bubble-wrap bags (Battery Network).
  • Do not mix loose lithium cells with coins, keys, or other metal in a pocket or container.
  • Call the retailer or check the locator listing for alkaline vs rechargeable vs e-bike acceptance before you drive.
  • MoCo: proof of residency and tape/bag rules live on the geo batteries page.

Not here / caution

  • A scraped list of Battery Network, Call2Recycle, or Earth911 stores (use the locators).
  • Invented national drop-off fees, star ratings, or “we tested” claims.
  • Damaged / swollen / overheating Li-ion or recalled packs in a standard retail collection box (Battery Network refuses; manufacturer path).
  • Wet-cell car batteries treated as dry-cell retail-box items.
  • Full MoCo HHW hours / ECOWISE fee table (that is the live geo page).
  • Meds / sharps pages (YMYL — not this site).
